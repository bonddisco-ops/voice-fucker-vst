VOICE FUCKER by Primal Rage Lab
===============================

WINDOWS
1. Copy "Voice Fucker.vst3" to:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
2. Rescan plugins in your DAW.
"Voice Fucker.exe" is the standalone version (no DAW needed).

MAC
1. VST3: copy "Voice Fucker.vst3" to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   AU (Logic, GarageBand): copy "Voice Fucker.component" to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   (In Finder press Shift+Cmd+G and paste the folder path.)
2. Open Terminal and run these two lines (otherwise macOS blocks the plugin):
   xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Voice Fucker.vst3"
   xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Voice Fucker.component"
3. Rescan plugins in your DAW. In Logic it appears under "MIDI-controlled Effects".

HOW TO FEED IT
- Put Voice Fucker on your vocal track.
- MIDI notes (Pitch = MIDI): in Bitwig add a Note Receiver before the plugin
  and pick the track with your notes. In Ableton set the MIDI track's
  "MIDI To" to the vocal track / Voice Fucker.
- Audio carrier (Source = Sidechain): route your synth into the plugin's
  sidechain input (Bitwig: sidechain selector in the plugin header).
- Pitch = Auto needs nothing: the robot follows the melody of your voice.
